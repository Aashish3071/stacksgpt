## The Problem

Keeping track of competitors means repeating the same searches for every rival: who they are, what they sell, what they charge and what customers say about them. It rarely gets done properly, so pricing and positioning decisions end up relying on old information.

## What You'll Get

- A list of companies similar to yours, found automatically.
- For each competitor: a company and funding overview, product and pricing notes, and customer review highlights.
- Everything saved in one Notion database your team can read and compare.

## How It Works

1. You tell the workflow which company is yours.
2. Exa, a search engine built for finding similar websites, returns a list of competitors.
3. Three AI researchers look into each competitor, and the combined report is saved to Notion.

## What You Need

- An Exa account
- A SerpApi account, which the AI researchers use to search the web
- An OpenAI account
- A Notion workspace
- An n8n account to run the workflow
- A weekend to set it up and review the first results

## Build It Step by Step

1. **Download the workflow file** from the box above and import it into n8n.
2. **Prepare Notion.** Create a database for competitors, add an integration in the Notion developer portal and share the database with it.
3. **Get your keys** for Exa, SerpApi and OpenAI.
4. **Connect everything** by adding the credentials in n8n and selecting your Notion database.
5. **Enter your company** in the step the template marks as required.
6. **Run it once** by hand and review the first entries in Notion before relying on them.

## Good to Know

- **Treat results as research notes.** Funding, pricing and review figures found online can be outdated or wrong, so check sources before sharing them.
- **It runs when you start it.** Add a schedule if you want the research refreshed regularly.
- **Skip known competitors.** Check Notion for an existing entry before researching the same company again.
- **Costs grow with your list.** Every competitor means more searches and AI calls.
- **Easy extras.** Add a step that alerts leadership in Slack when a competitor looks like a serious threat.

## Technical Details

```
[Manual Trigger: Your Company]
                     │
                     ▼
[Exa API: Find Similar Companies]
                     │
                     ▼
[Loop: One Competitor at a Time]
                     │
                     ▼
[Agent 1: Company and Funding Overview]
[Agent 2: Product Offering and Pricing]
[Agent 3: Customer Reviews]
                     │
                     ▼
[Combine Findings into a Report]
                     │
                     ▼
[Notion Node: Add Competitor Entry]
```

- **Discovery:** an HTTP request calls Exa's find similar endpoint with your company as the source.
- **Loop:** competitors are processed one at a time so a single failure does not stop the whole run.
- **Company overview agent:** searches sources such as Crunchbase, Wellfound and LinkedIn for founding details, key people, funding and news.
- **Product and review agents:** use SerpApi to find pages and a web scraping tool to read them.
- **Page size:** trim scraped pages before sending them to the model so long pages do not exceed its context window.
