## The Problem

New leads arrive through your website form, and someone has to read each one, look up the company and decide who deserves a quick call. Good leads wait in the queue while the team works through the rest, and by the time someone replies, the prospect may have moved on.

## What You'll Get

- Each lead scored against your ideal customer profile, with a short reason.
- A short summary of what the lead's company does, taken from their website.
- An instant Slack alert for your strongest leads, with the rest logged for follow-up.

## How It Works

1. A prospect fills in your website form.
2. AI summarizes their company website and scores the lead against rules you write.
3. The score and summary are saved to Google Sheets, and strong leads trigger a Slack alert.

## What You Need

- A website form, such as Typeform, Tally, Google Forms or your own
- A Google Sheet to keep track of leads
- An OpenAI account
- A Slack workspace
- An n8n account to run the workflow
- About half a day to set it up

## Build It Step by Step

1. **Download the building blocks** from the box above:
   - [Qualify New Leads in Google Sheets with OpenAI](/blueprints/templates/qualify-leads-google-sheets) handles the scoring.
   - [Enrich New Pipedrive Organizations and Notify Slack](/blueprints/templates/enrich-pipedrive-organizations) handles the website summary and the Slack message.
2. **Create your sheet** with columns for Timestamp, Name, Email, Company, Job Title, Use Case, Score and AI Summary.
3. **Start with the scoring template.** Point it at your sheet and paste the prompt below into the AI step.
4. **Add the website summary.** Copy the website scraping and summary steps from the second template into your workflow, before the scoring step. Use your form instead of the Pipedrive trigger.
5. **Add the alert.** Add a step that checks whether the score is above your chosen threshold, and send those leads to your Slack sales channel.
6. **Test it** by submitting a lead with a company email address and checking the sheet and Slack.

## Copy-Paste Prompt

Use this in the scoring step and replace the guidance with your own rules:

```text
You are an expert sales operations analyst scoring inbound prospects for a software service.

Review the following prospect submission:
Name: {{ $json.name }}
Email: {{ $json.email }}
Company: {{ $json.company }}
Role: {{ $json.role }}
Company website summary: {{ $json.websiteSummary }}
Problem Statement: {{ $json.message }}

Scoring guidance:
- Senior decision-makers such as founders, VPs, directors and heads of product score highest.
- Mid-level individual contributors score in the middle.
- A specific, urgent problem statement raises the score.
- Vague or student enquiries score low.
- Free webmail addresses lower the score.

Output format must be valid JSON:
{
  "score": <number from 0 to 100>,
  "companyTier": "Enterprise" | "MidMarket" | "SMB",
  "urgency": "High" | "Medium" | "Low",
  "executiveSummary": "<two sentence overview for the sales team>"
}

Only use the information provided. If something is unknown, say so instead of guessing.
```

## Good to Know

- **You own the rules.** Rewrite the scoring guidance whenever your ideal customer changes.
- **AI only knows what it is given.** The score is based on the form and the website. If you need verified company size or revenue, add a data provider such as Clearbit or Apollo.
- **Lead details go to OpenAI.** Only send the fields you need for scoring.
- **Keep spam out.** Add a hidden honeypot field to your form so bots are dropped before any AI step runs.
- **Easy extras.** Send your highest scoring leads a reply with your booking link, or create the contact straight in HubSpot or Salesforce.

## Technical Details

```
[Webhook / Form Trigger: New Inbound Lead]
                     │
                     ▼
[Domain Filter: Flag Free Webmail Domains]
                     │
                     ▼
[Website Summary + OpenAI: Score Against ICP]
                     │
                     ▼
[Google Sheets: Append Profile, Score & Reasoning]
                     │
                     ▼
[IF Condition: Score Above Your Threshold]
         ├── Yes ──► [Slack: Alert Account Executives Channel]
         └── No  ──► [CRM / Nurture: Log for Email Follow-Up]
```

The excerpt below shows the scoring and routing steps. It is not a complete workflow. Use a structured output parser so the score is always a number the IF step can compare.

```json
{
  "name": "Lead Qualification and Enrichment (excerpt)",
  "nodes": [
    {
      "name": "Inbound Lead Webhook",
      "type": "n8n-nodes-base.webhook",
      "typeVersion": 2,
      "position": [200, 300],
      "parameters": {
        "httpMethod": "POST",
        "path": "inbound-lead",
        "responseMode": "onReceived"
      }
    },
    {
      "name": "Score Lead with OpenAI",
      "type": "@n8n/n8n-nodes-langchain.openAi",
      "typeVersion": 1.8,
      "position": [440, 300],
      "parameters": {
        "messages": {
          "values": [
            {
              "role": "system",
              "content": "You are a B2B sales development representative. Evaluate the inbound prospect against our ICP. Return a valid JSON object containing: score, companyTier, urgency and executiveSummary."
            }
          ]
        }
      }
    },
    {
      "name": "Log to Google Sheets",
      "type": "n8n-nodes-base.googleSheets",
      "typeVersion": 4.5,
      "position": [680, 300],
      "parameters": {
        "operation": "append",
        "documentId": "={{ $vars.LEADS_SHEET_ID }}",
        "sheetName": "InboundLeads"
      }
    },
    {
      "name": "Check Priority Threshold",
      "type": "n8n-nodes-base.if",
      "typeVersion": 2,
      "position": [900, 300],
      "parameters": {
        "conditions": {
          "number": [
            {
              "value1": "={{ $json.score }}",
              "operation": "largerEqual",
              "value2": "={{ $vars.LEAD_SCORE_THRESHOLD }}"
            }
          ]
        }
      }
    }
  ]
}
```
