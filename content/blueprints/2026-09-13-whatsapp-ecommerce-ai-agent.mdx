## The Problem

Customers message your shop on WhatsApp asking about products, stock and delivery. Replying by hand means slow answers outside working hours, and interested buyers drift away or orders slip through before anyone writes them down.

## What You'll Get

- A WhatsApp assistant that answers product and policy questions from your own product list.
- A stock check before it recommends an item or confirms an order.
- Customer names and delivery addresses collected, with confirmed orders recorded in a Google Sheet.

## How It Works

1. A customer messages your WhatsApp Business number.
2. The AI assistant looks up your products, stock levels and FAQs in a Google Sheet.
3. It answers the question, or confirms the order and saves it to your Orders sheet.

## What You Need

- A Meta developer account with the WhatsApp Cloud API set up for your business number
- A Google Sheet with tabs for Products, Inventory, FAQ and Orders
- An OpenAI or Anthropic account
- An n8n account to run the workflow
- A public web address for n8n, so WhatsApp can send it messages
- About half a day to set it up

## Build It Step by Step

1. **Get the project.** The complete project, including its workflows, is in the WhatsApp eCommerce AI Agent repository listed under Sources and documentation below.
2. **Set up WhatsApp.** In the Meta App Dashboard, add the WhatsApp product, generate a permanent access token, note your Phone Number ID, set your webhook address and verify token, and subscribe to the `messages` field.
3. **Create your Google Sheet** with these tabs and columns:
   - **Products:** `SKU`, `Name`, `Price`, `Category`, `Description`
   - **Inventory:** `SKU`, `InStock`, `Reserved`
   - **FAQ:** `Topic`, `Question`, `Answer`
   - **Orders:** `OrderID`, `CreatedAt`, `Phone`, `CustomerName`, `Items`, `TotalAmount`, `PaymentMethod`, `ShippingAddress`, `Status`
4. **Import the workflow** from the repository into n8n and connect your AI model and Google Sheets credentials.
5. **Test it.** From a personal phone, message your test number with a product question and check that the assistant looks up your Products sheet before answering.

## Copy-Paste Prompt

Use this as the assistant's instructions:

```text
You are the lead sales executive for our online store on WhatsApp.
Your job is to assist customers politely, provide accurate pricing and product descriptions, check stock availability, and collect shipping information for confirmed orders.

Operating Rules:
1. Always look up prices and descriptions using your Product Search tool. Never guess prices.
2. Verify stock with the Inventory tool before confirming any order.
3. When the customer confirms an order, ask for their Full Name and Delivery Address.
4. Once you receive the shipping address, summarize the order and invoke the CreateOrder tool.
5. If the customer asks about returns, shipping times, or payment methods, consult your FAQ tool.
6. Keep replies concise and friendly, suitable for WhatsApp messaging. Use short paragraphs.
```

## Good to Know

- **It never takes card details.** The assistant shares payment links or bank transfer instructions, or records cash on delivery.
- **Prices come from your sheet.** The assistant reads prices and cannot change them or invent discounts.
- **People can take over.** If a customer is frustrated or asks for a person, mark the chat for human takeover and alert your team in Slack or Telegram.
- **Keep Google access stable.** Use a Google Cloud service account so the connection to your sheet does not expire.
- **Get told when it breaks.** Add an error alert in n8n for failed AI or WhatsApp requests.
- **Related workflow files.** See [WhatsApp Business Chatbot Grounded in Your Documents](/blueprints/templates/whatsapp-business-rag-chatbot) and [WooCommerce Customer Support Agent](/blueprints/templates/woocommerce-support-agent).

## Technical Details

The system combines a webhook verification server with n8n workflows. The reference repository verifies Meta's `X-Hub-Signature-256` header with HMAC-SHA256 and rejects unsigned requests. The layout below is an example; the repository organizes its files in its own way.

```text
whatsapp-ecommerce-agent/
├── server/
│   ├── index.js          # Webhook signature verification and deduplication
│   ├── meta-verify.js    # Hub mode challenge responder
│   └── package.json      # Dependencies (Express, crypto)
├── n8n-workflows/
│   ├── whatsapp-sales-agent.json # Sales agent workflow
│   └── sheets-sync.json          # Google Sheets order dispatcher
└── sheets/
    ├── Products.csv
    ├── Inventory.csv
    ├── FAQ.csv
    └── Orders.csv
```

Normalizing an incoming WhatsApp message in an n8n Code node:

```javascript
const body = $input.first().json.body;
const entry = body.entry?.[0];
const changes = entry?.changes?.[0];
const value = changes?.value;
const message = value?.messages?.[0];

if (!message) {
  return [];
}

return [{
  json: {
    messageId: message.id,
    from: message.from,
    timestamp: message.timestamp,
    text: message.text?.body || "",
    contactName: value?.contacts?.[0]?.profile?.name || "Customer"
  }
}];
```
