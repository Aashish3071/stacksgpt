## The Problem

Seeing what competitors are running means scrolling through Meta's Ad Library and taking screenshots. Turning that into fresh ideas for your own ads means briefing a designer from scratch every time, so the research rarely becomes something you can test.

## What You'll Get

- Your competitors' active image ads collected and saved in organized Google Drive folders.
- A detailed description of each ad's imagery, layout and message.
- Three new style variations of each ad created with AI and logged in a Google Sheet for review.

## How It Works

1. You search the Meta Ad Library for a competitor or a keyword.
2. The workflow collects the active image ads and AI describes each one.
3. AI creates new versions in different styles and saves them to Google Drive and Google Sheets.

## What You Need

- An Apify account, which collects the ads
- An OpenAI account with access to image generation
- Google Drive and Google Sheets
- An n8n account to run the workflow
- A weekend to set it up and test it

## Build It Step by Step

1. **Download the workflow file** from the box above and import it into n8n.
2. **Find your search.** Open the Meta Ad Library, search for a competitor or keyword and copy the page address.
3. **Run the setup part once.** It creates your Google Sheet and main Google Drive folder. Copy their IDs into the Set Variables step.
4. **Add your details.** Paste your Apify key and search address into the ad scraper step, and connect OpenAI, Google Drive and Google Sheets.
5. **Start small.** Keep the batch limit low for your first run.
6. **Run it** and review the new variations in Google Drive.

## Copy-Paste Prompt

Use this in the step that plans each variation:

```text
You are an elite direct-response creative strategist.
Review the competitor ad description provided.
Identify the primary emotional hook and value proposition.
Write three distinct change requests an AI image editor can apply for our brand.
Keep element placement the same, but change the color palette, typography style and primary headline.
```

## Good to Know

- **Use competitors for inspiration, not copies.** Do not reuse their trademarks, product claims or protected designs.
- **Your team picks what to test.** Review every variation for brand fit before it goes near an ad account.
- **Costs grow per image.** Every variation is an image generation request, so keep batches small.
- **Scrapers can break.** If Meta changes its pages, the collection step may fail. Turn on error alerts in n8n.
- **Tidy up storage.** Archive older folders in Google Drive from time to time.

## Technical Details

```
[Apify Actor: Scrape Meta Ad Library]
                  │
                  ▼
[Filter & Limit: Image Ads Only, Small Batch]
                  │
                  ▼
[Google Drive: Create Source and Variation Folders]
                  │
                  ▼
[OpenAI Vision: Describe the Ad]
                  │
                  ▼
[AI Prompt Step: Write Three Style Change Requests]
                  │
                  ▼
[OpenAI Image Editing → Google Drive + Google Sheets]
```

- **Collection:** an HTTP request runs an Apify Ad Library actor; a filter removes ads without images and a limit sets the batch size.
- **Folders:** each ad gets a parent folder named after its archive ID, with source and variation subfolders.
- **Analysis:** OpenAI Vision describes the image, and an AI step writes three change requests.
- **Generation:** each change request is sent to OpenAI's image editing endpoint, then uploaded to Google Drive and logged in Google Sheets.
