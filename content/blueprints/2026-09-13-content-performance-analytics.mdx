## The Problem

Knowing how your website is doing means logging into Google Analytics, finding the right report and working out what actually changed. Most weeks it gets skipped, so a drop in traffic is noticed late, and nobody can say whether a busy week was unusual or just normal for the time of year.

## What You'll Get

- A weekly report in your inbox comparing the last seven days with the same week last year.
- A short explanation of the biggest changes, written in plain words by AI.
- A shorter version sent to Telegram for anyone who only wants the headline.

## How It Works

1. Every week, the workflow pulls your website traffic numbers from Google Analytics.
2. It pulls the same week from last year so the comparison is fair.
3. AI writes a short report with the changes and sends it by email and Telegram.

## What You Need

- A Google Analytics property for your website
- An OpenAI account
- An email account that can send mail through SMTP
- A Telegram bot, if you want the short version
- An n8n account to run the workflow
- About half a day to set it up

## Build It Step by Step

1. **Download the workflow file** from the box above and import it into n8n.
2. **Connect Google Analytics.** Create a Google credential in n8n that can read your property.
3. **Connect OpenAI, email and Telegram** by adding their credentials in n8n.
4. **Choose your property** in both Google Analytics steps.
5. **Set who receives it.** Enter the email recipients and your Telegram chat ID.
6. **Pick the day.** The template runs on Monday morning. Change the schedule if another day suits you.
7. **Test it.** Run the workflow once by hand and check the report before activating it.

## Copy-Paste Prompt

Use this in the analysis step:

```text
You are an expert content analytics consultant.
You will receive Google Analytics metrics for the last seven days and for the same seven days last year.

Structure your response into these sections:
1. Summary: two sentences on the overall change.
2. Comparison table: each metric with both periods and the percentage change.
3. What changed: the biggest gains and drops, with possible explanations clearly marked as hypotheses.
4. Suggested checks: specific things the team should look into this week.

Only use the figures provided. Do not invent numbers.
```

## Good to Know

- **The report explains, your team decides.** It highlights what changed; people choose what to do about it.
- **Year on year needs history.** The comparison only works if your property has data for the same week last year.
- **Rename the steps.** Some steps in the template have German names. Rename them so your team can maintain the workflow.
- **Get told when it breaks.** Add an error alert so you know if Google access expires.
- **Prefer Slack?** Add a Slack step to post the report to your team channel.

## Technical Details

```
[Schedule Trigger: Weekly]
                    │
                    ▼
[Google Analytics: Last Seven Days] ──► [Summarize]
                    │
                    ▼
[Google Analytics: Same Days Last Year] ──► [Summarize]
                    │
                    ▼
[OpenAI: Comparison Table and Analysis]
          ├── Branch 1 ──► [Email: Full Report]
          └── Branch 2 ──► [OpenAI: Short Version] ──► [Telegram]
```

- **Data pulls:** two Google Analytics report steps, one for the last seven days and one for the same dates in the previous year, each followed by a summarize step.
- **Analysis:** an OpenAI step builds the comparison table and commentary as HTML for email.
- **Short version:** a second OpenAI step converts the HTML into plain text for Telegram.
