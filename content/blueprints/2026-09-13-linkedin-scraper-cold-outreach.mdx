## The Problem

Building a list of prospects means searching a lead database, copying names and job titles into a spreadsheet, looking up each company and writing an opening line for every person. It takes hours before a single email goes out, so outreach happens in bursts instead of steadily.

## What You'll Get

- A list of people who match the job titles, company size and location you describe.
- A short research summary of each person's company.
- A personalized opening message for each lead, added to your cold email campaign and ready for review.

## How It Works

1. You fill in a short form describing the people you want to reach.
2. The workflow finds matching leads, tidies up their details and saves them to a Google Sheet.
3. AI researches each company, writes a personalized message and adds the lead to your Instantly campaign.

## What You Need

- An Apify account, which runs the lead scraper
- An OpenAI account
- A Tavily account for company research
- An Instantly account with a campaign set up
- A Google Sheet for your leads
- An n8n account to run the workflow
- A weekend to set it up and test it

## Build It Step by Step

1. **Download the workflow file** from the box above and import it into n8n.
2. **Create your accounts** for Apify, OpenAI, Tavily and Instantly, and copy each API key.
3. **Prepare your sheet.** Create a Google Sheet with columns such as `Full Name`, `Email`, `Title`, `LinkedIn`, `Company Name` and `Company Website`.
4. **Add your keys.** Connect OpenAI and Google Sheets in n8n, then paste your Apify and Instantly keys into the steps that call those services.
5. **Pick your sheet and campaign** in the matching steps.
6. **Run a small test.** Submit the form with a narrow search, then check the leads in your sheet and the messages in Instantly.
7. **Review before sending.** Read the generated messages in Instantly and adjust the prompt until the tone sounds like you.

## Copy-Paste Prompt

Use this in the message writing step to shape your opening lines:

```text
Review the prospect's background: {{ $json.summary }}
Company website: {{ $json.companyWebsite }}
Write one sentence with a personalized observation about their company's recent focus.
Keep it conversational and natural, and avoid generic praise or buzzwords.
```

## Good to Know

- **Nothing sends until you say so.** Leads land in your Instantly campaign, where you review the messages and settings before launching.
- **Check the terms first.** Collecting Apollo data through a third party scraper may conflict with Apollo's terms of service.
- **Protect your sender reputation.** Verify email addresses before sending, and warm up new sending domains.
- **Avoid duplicates.** Use the email address or LinkedIn URL as a unique key so the same person is not added twice.
- **Check current prices.** Apify, Tavily and Instantly are paid services, and OpenAI bills per use.

## Technical Details

```
[Form: Job Title, Company Size, Keywords, Location]
                   │
                   ▼
[AI: Build Apollo Search URL]
                   │
                   ▼
[Apify Actor: Scrape Apollo Results]
                   │
                   ▼
[OpenAI: Parse Lead Data] ──► [Google Sheets: Save Lead]
                   │
                   ▼
[AI Agent with Tavily: Research Company]
                   │
                   ▼
[OpenAI: Write Outreach Message]
                   │
                   ▼
[Instantly: Add Lead to Campaign]
```

- **Lead search:** an AI step turns the form answers into an Apollo search URL, which an Apify actor scrapes through an HTTP request.
- **Lead parsing:** OpenAI converts each raw result into consistent fields before the lead is appended to Google Sheets.
- **Company research:** an AI agent uses the Tavily search tool to summarize the company.
- **Campaign hand-off:** the lead and message are sent to Instantly's API through an HTTP request.
