Anthropic has expanded its educational portal, Claude Academy, with a practical workflow framework titled "Explore what Claude can do for you." The resource aims to solve one of the most common friction points in workplace artificial intelligence adoption: knowledge workers opening a blank chat interface, asking isolated questions, and abandoning the tool because they do not know how to connect it to their actual responsibilities.

The new onboarding module shifts the starting interaction from open prompt engineering to role description. Users begin by detailing their primary job function, typical responsibilities, and ongoing projects. For example, a product manager can describe working on software roadmap prioritization and customer research. Based on this profile, Claude generates five concrete, immediate exercises tailored to the role, rather than generic writing samples or trivia.

A central element of the guided workflow is optional document grounding. The framework integrates with external storage connectors, notably Google Drive, allowing Claude to read working documents directly without requiring manual cut-and-paste uploads. When linked to genuine work assets, the model references existing project files to generate contextual deliverables, such as extracting friction points from customer interview notes, pressure-testing product trade-offs, or converting rough observations into one-page executive decision memos.

Anthropic did not publish a specific creation date on the Academy portal, and access was catalogued in September 2026. The onboarding guide is available to anyone with a standard Claude.ai account, requiring roughly fifteen minutes to complete. By focusing on existing workflows and live documents, the tool aims to make artificial intelligence directly relevant to non-technical operators, students, and small business leads.
