At its annual global technology conference, NVIDIA announced a broad partnership with leading industrial software providers, including Cadence, Dassault Systèmes, PTC, Siemens, and Synopsys, to integrate autonomous AI agents and physical digital twins directly into core engineering workflows. The initiative aims to modernize how complex industrial products, automotive systems, and advanced semiconductor chips are designed, simulated, and manufactured.

Industrial engineering has reached an agentic inflection point. While previous computing waves accelerated numerical simulations and 3D modeling, modern engineering suites require teams of specialized engineers to manually orchestrate verification passes, adjust manufacturing tolerances, and interpret vast telemetry streams from automated assembly lines.

### Autonomous agents for silicon design and physical simulation

By combining NVIDIA accelerated computing, CUDA-X libraries, NVIDIA NeMo agent frameworks, and Nemotron open models, industrial software vendors are embedding domain-specific AI agents into their flagship software suites:

* **Semiconductor Verification**: Cadence introduced ChipStack AI SuperAgent, integrating electronic design automation (EDA) software with agentic orchestration to automate testbench coding, design verification, and automated debugging for next-generation silicon architectures.
* **Product Lifecycle & Virtual Twins**: Dassault Systèmes is embedding role-based Virtual Twin agents into its 3DEXPERIENCE platform, allowing design teams to continuously optimize product geometry against real-world mechanical stress.
* **Robotics Simulation**: PTC announced a direct bridge from its cloud-native Onshape computer-aided design (CAD) platform to NVIDIA Isaac Sim, enabling robotics manufacturers like FANUC America to validate robotic kinematics within physically accurate OpenUSD virtual environments before commissioning physical factory lines.

### Global industrial adoption across manufacturing leaders

The agentic software stack is already seeing rapid implementation across major international manufacturing leaders. TSMC, Samsung, and SK hynix are deploying accelerated EDA agents to shorten semiconductor fabrication cycles, while automotive manufacturers including Mercedes-Benz, Honda, and Jaguar Land Rover utilize Omniverse simulations to plan flexible assembly facilities.

The industrial agent solutions are available across leading hyperscale cloud infrastructure providers, including Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, as well as enterprise on-premises server systems from Dell Technologies, HPE, and Supermicro.
